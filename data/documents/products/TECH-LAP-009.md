# Dell Inspiron 14

## Product Overview

The Dell Inspiron 14 is a balanced 14-inch laptop for development, office work, and travel. It sits between value and premium portable options in the managed demo catalog.

## Key Specifications

- **Processor:** Intel Core i5 class processor
- **Memory:** 16GB RAM
- **Storage:** 512GB SSD
- **Display:** 14-inch display
- **Graphics:** Integrated graphics
- **Weight:** 1.45 kg
- **Use Cases:** Office, coding, and travel

## Compatibility

Suitable for software development, productivity, and everyday travel. It is not intended for discrete-GPU gaming workloads.

## Common Questions

**Q: What is the main trade-off?**

A: It balances memory, portability, and price rather than maximizing gaming GPU performance or ultra-light weight.
