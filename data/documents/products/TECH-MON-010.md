# AOC 24 Office Monitor

## Product Overview

The AOC 24 Office Monitor is an affordable IPS display for office productivity, study, and general desktop work.

## Key Specifications

- **Screen Size:** 24 inches
- **Resolution:** 1920 x 1080 (1080p)
- **Panel Type:** IPS
- **Refresh Rate:** 75Hz
- **Use Cases:** Office

## Compatibility

Suitable for common HDMI/DisplayPort desktop and laptop connections and everyday productivity work.

## Common Questions

**Q: Is it a high-refresh gaming monitor?**

A: No. Its 75Hz refresh rate and 1080p resolution target office value rather than competitive gaming.
