# ASUS Zenbook 14 OLED

## Product Overview

The ASUS Zenbook 14 OLED is a lightweight development and travel laptop with a 14-inch OLED display, balanced memory/storage, and a portable chassis.

## Key Specifications

- **Processor:** Intel Core i7 class processor
- **Memory:** 16GB RAM
- **Storage:** 512GB SSD
- **Display:** 14-inch OLED display
- **Graphics:** Integrated graphics
- **Weight:** 1.28 kg
- **Use Cases:** Office, coding, and travel

## Compatibility

Suitable for software development, office applications, and travel use. It is not positioned as a discrete-GPU gaming laptop.

## Common Questions

**Q: Is it portable enough for daily travel?**

A: Its 1.28 kg chassis and 14-inch size are intended for portable development and office work.
