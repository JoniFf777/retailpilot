# Acer Aspire 5

## Product Overview

The Acer Aspire 5 is an affordable everyday laptop for study, browsing, and office productivity. Its Ryzen 5 processor and 15.6-inch display provide practical value for users who prioritize price and storage.

## Key Specifications

- **Processor:** AMD Ryzen 5 mobile processor
- **Memory:** 8GB RAM
- **Storage:** 512GB SSD
- **Display:** 15.6-inch display
- **Graphics:** Integrated graphics
- **Weight:** 1.80 kg
- **Use Cases:** Office and study

## Compatibility

Runs common office, browser, video-conference, and study applications on Windows. The integrated graphics are intended for everyday work rather than demanding gaming.

## Common Questions

**Q: Is this suitable for basic study and office work?**

A: Yes. It is positioned as a value laptop for everyday productivity and study.
