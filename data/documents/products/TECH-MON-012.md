# ASUS TUF Gaming 27

## Product Overview

The ASUS TUF Gaming 27 is a practical 27-inch gaming display with 1440p resolution and a fast IPS refresh rate at a mid-range price.

## Key Specifications

- **Screen Size:** 27 inches
- **Resolution:** 2560 x 1440 (1440p)
- **Panel Type:** IPS
- **Refresh Rate:** 170Hz
- **Use Cases:** Gaming and office

## Compatibility

Designed for desktop and laptop gaming setups that support 1440p high-refresh output. It also works for ordinary office use.

## Common Questions

**Q: Is it a lower-cost alternative to a premium 4K gaming display?**

A: Yes. It emphasizes 1440p high-refresh gaming at a lower managed-catalog price point.
