# LG UltraGear OLED 27

## Product Overview

The LG UltraGear OLED 27 is a premium high-refresh monitor for gaming and visually demanding work, combining 4K resolution with an OLED panel.

## Key Specifications

- **Screen Size:** 27 inches
- **Resolution:** 3840 x 2160 (4K)
- **Panel Type:** OLED
- **Refresh Rate:** 240Hz
- **Use Cases:** Gaming and design

## Compatibility

Best suited to systems with a graphics output capable of driving high-resolution, high-refresh content.
